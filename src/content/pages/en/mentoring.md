Would you like to share your knowledge with young coders?

Become a volunteer mentor at CoderDojo Schöneweide!

* Every 2nd Saturday, or flexibly based on your availability
* Always as part of a team with other mentors

Curious? Just email us at engagieren@coderdojo-schoeneweide.de

## Requirements
* No prior knowledge needed - just a willingness to learn.
* Onboarding sessions (basic training on mentoring and child and youth protection) take place, by arrangement, on Wednesdays from 17:30 - 19:00. Attendance is mandatory.
* In order to volunteer with us as a mentor, we need to review your extended police clearance certificate. It's free. We'll help you apply for it!
* All our mentors commit to our Code of Conduct.
